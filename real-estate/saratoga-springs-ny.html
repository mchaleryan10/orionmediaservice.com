Why Saratoga Sellers Choose Orion

Saratoga Springs is one of the most competitive real estate markets in Upstate New York. With a median home price well above the Capital Region average — driven by the historic racing season, a thriving downtown, and proximity to the Adirondacks — sellers here can't afford average listing photos. Buyers arrive from New York City, Boston, and beyond, often making decisions based on online listings before ever visiting in person.

We've photographed properties across Saratoga Springs and Saratoga County — from the grand North Broadway estates with their sweeping front porches and mature elms, to the waterfront cottages on Lake Lonely and Saratoga Lake, to the contemporary builds along the Wilton corridor. Each property tells a different story, and our job is to tell it in a way that stops a buyer mid-scroll.

Racing season (July–August) brings an influx of luxury buyers to Saratoga, many looking for investment properties and second homes. North Broadway and Circular Street listings that hit the market with professional drone aerials during racing season routinely generate multiple offers. A well-photographed listing is your single highest-ROI marketing investment.
