Why Troy Realtors & Investors Choose Orion

Troy's historic streetscapes represent one of the most visually stunning and rapidly evolving real estate markets in the Capital Region. From ornate 19th-century Victorian brownstones bordering Washington Park and the Downtown Waterfront District to multi-family rental properties in South Troy and contemporary lofts overlooking the Hudson River, Troy listings demand specialized architectural photography that balances deep shadows and period woodwork with bright outdoor views.

With ongoing revitalization and strong influx from NYC and regional investors drawn to Troy's walkable downtown, arts scene, and university proximity (Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute and Russell Sage College), online listing presentation is the critical differentiator. Listings featuring high-dynamic-range photography and FAA drone aerials earn dramatically higher saves on Zillow and Redfin and generate competitive multi-offer bidding.

Headquartered in Cohoes, Orion Media is literally right across the river — just a five-minute drive across the 112th Street Bridge or Ontario Street. We offer same-week shoot availability, rapid rescheduling when Upstate weather turns, and guaranteed next-day delivery with zero travel fees anywhere in Troy or Rensselaer County.
