Why Latham Real Estate Agents Partner With Orion Media

Situated immediately southwest of Cohoes, Latham is the vibrant residential and commercial hub of Albany County. Renowned for the prestigious North Colonie Central School District (anchored by Shaker High School), Latham features an exceptional volume of single-family homes — ranging from executive custom estates bordering Loudonville to classic suburban developments in Forts Ferry and newer construction off Boght Road.

Because of its central location at the intersection of I-87 and Route 7, homes in Latham move at a rapid pace and attract strong buyer demand from healthcare professionals, state workers, and tech employees. Listings that debut with professional HDR photography and FAA-certified drone aerials capture significantly more views on Zillow and Realtor.com, resulting in faster showings and higher sale-to-list price ratios.

Being located right next door in Cohoes, Orion Media is virtually in Latham's backyard. We provide unmatched same-week availability, rapid response for weather reschedules, guaranteed 24-hour turnaround on HDR photo packages, and zero travel fees.
