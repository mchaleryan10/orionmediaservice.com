Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking videography, photography, and drone services with Orion Media.

What areas of the Capital Region do you serve?

Orion Media is centrally located in Cohoes, NY. We provide full photography and videography coverage across Albany, Saratoga Springs, Troy, Schenectady, Clifton Park, Colonie, Latham, Guilderland, and surrounding communities with zero travel fees. We also regularly travel to Lake George, the Adirondacks, and the Hudson Valley.

What is the turnaround time for completed projects?

Real estate photography and 3D Matterport tours are delivered within 24 to 48 hours so agents can launch listings immediately. For wedding videography and photography, sneak peek galleries and teasers are delivered within 3 to 5 days, while fully edited highlight films and master galleries are delivered in 4 to 6 weeks.

Are your drone operations licensed and insured?

Yes. Founder Ryan McHale is an FAA Part 107 certified commercial drone pilot. We operate fully registered aircraft and carry a $2,000,000 aviation liability insurance policy, ensuring every aerial shoot meets strict federal safety guidelines.

Can we bundle multiple services together?

Absolutely. Bundling services is our most popular option. Real estate agents frequently bundle HDR photos, drone aerials, and 3D Matterport walkthroughs for maximum savings. Wedding couples often bundle full-day photo coverage, cinematic highlight film, and drone footage.

How do we reserve our date and book?

Simply fill out our quote form or call/text us directly at (518) 222-9845. We will verify date availability, review your creative requirements, and provide a transparent written proposal. Dates are secured with a simple digital agreement and deposit.
